## 🔬 Selected Engineering Projects

I believe the best way to understand engineering is to build things. Most of my practical experience has come from working on projects that combine multiple engineering disciplines to address real-world problems.

### 1. AutoMed – Intelligent Medication Management System

An IoT- and AI-powered medication management solution designed to address medication non-adherence by automating medication dispensing and improving communication between patients and caregivers.

**My Contributions:**
- Developed the embedded hardware infrastructure.
- Programmed the ESP32 microcontroller.
- Integrated electronic components and the medication dispensing mechanism.
- Developed the interface between the hardware and software systems.
- Worked on a servo-driven medication carousel with a 14-compartment configuration.
- Contributed to hardware integration, user research, and prototype development.

**Technologies:** C++, ESP32, Embedded Systems, Servo Motors, RTC, IoT.

### 2. SAOT – Sustainable Agriculture Optimization Tool

An AI- and IoT-powered agricultural platform designed to help farmers make data-driven decisions through intelligent monitoring and agricultural optimization.

The project focuses on improving agricultural productivity through the integration of environmental sensors, automation, and AI-assisted insights.

**Key Features:**
- Soil moisture, temperature, and pH monitoring.
- Soil nutrient monitoring and visualization.
- Automated irrigation.
- AI-assisted pest and disease identification.
- Animal intrusion monitoring.
- Agricultural data analytics and decision support.

**Technologies:** C++, ESP32, Arduino, IoT, Environmental Sensors, AI, Mobile and Web Technologies.

### 3. SAEMS – Smart Autonomous Environmental Monitoring System

An autonomous environmental monitoring robot designed to identify potentially hazardous environmental conditions through continuous monitoring and sensor integration.

**Key Features:**
- Autonomous movement and line-following capabilities.
- Environmental monitoring.
- Smoke and flame detection.
- Harmful gas detection.
- Temperature and humidity monitoring.
- Real-time monitoring and alerting.

**Technologies:** ESP32, Arduino, C++, Sensors, Embedded Systems, React Native, Firebase.

### 4. SP3 – Sustainable Solar Food Preservation System

A solar-powered food preservation concept designed to reduce post-harvest losses by improving access to sustainable food preservation technology.

The project explores the application of renewable energy, thermal management, and engineering design to address food preservation challenges.

**Focus Areas:** Renewable Energy, Embedded Systems, Temperature Monitoring, Sustainable Engineering.
